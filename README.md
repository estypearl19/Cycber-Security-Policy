# Estech Company Cybersecurity Policy

 **Document Title:** Estech Company Cybersecurity Policy\
 **Document Owner:** IT/Cybersecurity Department\
 **Approved By:** Senior Management\
 **Version:** 1.0\
 **Effective Date:** 29/09/2026 \
 **Review Date:** 29/03/2027 \
 **Classification:** Internal Use

 ## 1\. Purpose

 The purpose of this Cybersecurity Policy is to establish the rules, responsibilities, and controls required to protect **Estech Company's** information, computer systems, networks, applications, devices, and other digital assets from cybersecurity threats.

 This policy aims to:

 - Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company information.
- Reduce the risk of cyberattacks, data breaches, malware, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Establish clear cybersecurity responsibilities for employees, contractors, and management.
- Ensure that cybersecurity incidents are identified, reported, investigated, and handled promptly.
- Promote a strong cybersecurity culture throughout Estech.

 ## 2\. Scope

 This policy applies to:

 - All Estech employees, whether permanent, temporary, or contract staff.
- Consultants, contractors, interns, and third-party service providers who access Estech systems.
- All company-owned or authorized personal devices used to access company information.
- Company networks, servers, cloud services, applications, databases, websites, and communication systems.
- All company information, regardless of whether it is stored electronically, on paper, or in cloud systems.

 Everyone covered by this policy is responsible for protecting Estech's information and technology resources.

 ## 3\. Cybersecurity Principles

 Estech will follow these fundamental security principles:

 1. **Confidentiality** – Information must only be accessible to authorized individuals.
2. **Integrity** – Information and systems must be protected against unauthorized modification or destruction.
3. **Availability** – Authorized users should have reliable access to systems and information when required.
4. **Least privilege** – Users receive only the access necessary to perform their duties.
5. **Defense in depth** – Multiple security controls should be used to reduce the impact of a single control failing.
6. **Risk-based security** – Security resources should be prioritized according to identified risks.

 ## 4\. Roles and Responsibilities

 ### 4.1 Senior Management

 Senior management shall:

 - Approve and support the cybersecurity policy.
- Provide appropriate resources for cybersecurity.
- Ensure significant cybersecurity risks are addressed.
- Promote cybersecurity awareness across the organization.

 ### 4.2 IT/Cybersecurity Department

 The IT/Cybersecurity Department shall:

 - Implement and maintain security controls.
- Monitor networks and systems for suspicious activity.
- Manage security vulnerabilities and software updates.
- Conduct security assessments and risk reviews.
- Manage cybersecurity incidents.
- Maintain backups and disaster-recovery arrangements.
- Provide cybersecurity awareness training.

 ### 4.3 Employees and Contractors

 Users shall:

 - Protect their passwords and authentication credentials.
- Follow Estech's security procedures.
- Use company systems only for authorized purposes.
- Report suspicious emails, activities, or security incidents promptly.
- Keep company devices secure.
- Complete required cybersecurity training.
- Never intentionally bypass security controls.

 ## 5\. Access Control

 Access to Estech systems shall be granted based on job responsibilities and business requirements.

 - User accounts must be unique to individual users.
- Shared accounts should be avoided unless technically necessary and specifically authorized.
- Access rights shall follow the **principle of least privilege**.
- Privileged/administrator accounts shall be restricted to authorized personnel.
- Access rights shall be reviewed periodically.
- Accounts belonging to employees who leave Estech shall be disabled promptly.
- Temporary access shall have an appropriate expiration date.

 ## 6\. Password and Authentication Policy

 All users must follow these requirements:

 - Use strong, unique passwords for company accounts.
- Do not share passwords with other people.
- Do not write passwords where unauthorized people can access them.
- Do not reuse company passwords on personal websites or services.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be enabled for systems that support it, particularly email, remote access, administrative accounts, and critical applications.
- Suspected compromised passwords must be changed immediately and reported to IT/Cybersecurity.

 Where technically possible, Estech should use password managers and centralized identity management.

 ## 7. Acceptable Use of IT Resources

 Company computers, networks, internet connections, email systems, cloud services, and other technology resources must be used responsibly and legally.

 Users must not:

 - Access systems without authorization.
- Download or install unauthorized software.
- Disable antivirus, endpoint protection, firewalls, or other security controls.
- Use company systems to conduct illegal activities.
- Attempt to bypass access controls.
- Introduce malware or other malicious software.
- Share confidential company information with unauthorized individuals.
- Use company resources to access or distribute inappropriate content.

 Limited personal use may be permitted where it does not interfere with business operations or violate company policies.

 ## 8\. Email and Phishing Security

 Employees must exercise caution when receiving emails, messages, or attachments.

 Users should:

 - Verify unexpected requests for sensitive information or payments.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments.
- Check the sender's address carefully.
- Be particularly cautious about urgent requests involving money, passwords, or confidential information.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the IT/Cybersecurity Department.

 Employees must not respond to suspected phishing messages using company credentials or provide confidential information without verification.

 ## 9\. Device and Endpoint Security

 All company devices must have appropriate security protections.

 Estech shall, where appropriate:

 - Deploy endpoint protection/anti-malware software.
- Enable firewalls.
- Apply security updates and patches promptly.
- Configure automatic screen locking.
- Encrypt sensitive information stored on portable devices where feasible.
- Maintain an inventory of company devices.
- Restrict unauthorized software installation.

 Employees must not leave company laptops, phones, or other devices unattended in insecure locations.

 ## 10\. Network Security

 Estech's networks shall be protected through appropriate technical controls, which may include:

 - Firewalls.
- Network segmentation.
- Secure wireless configuration.
- Intrusion detection/prevention systems.
- Secure remote-access solutions.
- Network monitoring and logging.
- Encryption of sensitive communications.

 Remote workers must use approved secure connections when accessing company resources.

 ## 11\. Data Protection and Classification

 Estech information shall be classified according to its sensitivity.

 A suggested classification system is:

 - **Public:** Information approved for public disclosure.
- **Internal:** Information intended for Estech employees and authorized users.
- **Confidential:** Sensitive business, customer, employee, financial, or operational information.
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive information requiring the highest level of protection.

 Confidential and Restricted information must only be shared with authorized individuals and through approved communication and storage systems.

 ## 12. Data Backup and Recovery

 Estech shall maintain appropriate backups of critical information and systems.

 Backups should:

 - Be performed according to documented schedules.
- Be protected against unauthorized access.
- Include appropriate encryption where necessary.
- Include protections against ransomware and accidental deletion.
- Be tested periodically to confirm that information can actually be restored.

 Critical systems shall have documented recovery procedures.

 ## 13\. Software and Patch Management

 Only approved and appropriately licensed software may be installed on company systems.

 The IT Department shall:

 - Maintain an inventory of software and systems.
- Monitor for security vulnerabilities.
- Apply security patches within risk-based timeframes.
- Prioritize critical vulnerabilities affecting internet-facing or business-critical systems.
- Remove unsupported software where practical.

 ## 14\. Mobile and Remote Working

 Employees working remotely must maintain the same level of security expected in the office.

 Users must:

 - Secure company devices when working outside the office.
- Avoid using unsecured public computers for company business.
- Use approved VPN or secure remote-access technologies where required.
- Avoid accessing sensitive information over insecure public networks unless appropriate security controls are in place.
- Report lost or stolen company devices immediately.

 ## 15\. Third-Party Security

 Third parties that access Estech systems or sensitive information must meet appropriate security requirements.

 Where appropriate, contracts should address:

 - Confidentiality.
- Data protection.
- Access controls.
- Security responsibilities.
- Incident notification.
- Data retention and deletion.
- Termination of access.

 Third-party access should be reviewed and removed when it is no longer required.

 ## 16\. Cybersecurity Incident Reporting

 Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents to the IT/Cybersecurity Department.

 Examples include:

 - Suspected phishing attacks.
- Malware infections.
- Lost or stolen company devices.
- Unauthorized account access.
- Accidental disclosure of confidential information.
- Suspicious system activity.
- Data breaches.
- Unusual login notifications.
- Ransomware or extortion attempts.

 Employees should **not attempt to investigate, delete evidence, or conceal an incident** unless specifically instructed by authorized personnel.

 ## 17\. Incident Response

 Estech shall maintain an incident-response process covering:

 1. **Identification** – Detect and confirm the incident.
2. **Containment** – Limit the spread or impact.
3. **Eradication** – Remove the threat and address its cause.
4. **Recovery** – Restore affected systems safely.
5. **Lessons learned** – Review the incident and improve controls.

 Significant incidents shall be escalated to senior management and other relevant parties according to the incident-response procedure.

 ## 18\. Security Awareness Training

 All employees shall receive cybersecurity awareness training:

 - During onboarding.
- Periodically during employment.
- When significant security threats or policy changes occur.

 Training should cover areas such as:

 - Password security.
- Phishing and social engineering.
- Safe internet use.
- Data protection.
- Device security.
- Incident reporting.
- Remote-working security.

 Estech may also conduct simulated phishing exercises and other awareness activities.

 ## 19\. Physical Security

 Physical access to offices, server rooms, network equipment, and other sensitive areas shall be restricted to authorized personnel.

 Employees must:

 - Keep identification/access cards secure.
- Challenge or report unauthorized persons in restricted areas where appropriate.
- Secure confidential documents.
- Follow clean-desk and clear-screen requirements where applicable.
- Properly dispose of sensitive documents and storage media.

 ## 20\. Monitoring and Logging

 Estech may monitor company systems, networks, and devices for legitimate security and operational purposes, subject to applicable laws and company requirements.

 Security logs should be protected from unauthorized modification and retained for an appropriate period.

 Monitoring may include:

 - Authentication events.
- Network activity.
- Endpoint security alerts.
- Administrative activity.
- Access to sensitive systems.
- Security incidents.

 ## 21\. Vulnerability Management and Security Testing

 Estech shall periodically assess its systems for security weaknesses.

 Activities may include:

 - Vulnerability scanning.
- Security configuration reviews.
- Penetration testing where appropriate.
- Risk assessments.
- Security audits.
- Review of emerging cybersecurity threats.

 Identified vulnerabilities shall be prioritized and remediated according to their risk.

 ## 22\. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

 Estech shall maintain appropriate business continuity and disaster-recovery arrangements for critical systems.

 Plans should address scenarios such as:

 - Cyberattacks.
- Ransomware.
- Major system failures.
- Data loss.
- Power or infrastructure failures.
- Significant disruption to company facilities.

 Recovery procedures should be tested periodically.

 ## 23\. Policy Violations

 Failure to comply with this policy may expose Estech to cybersecurity, financial, legal, and reputational risks.

 Violations may result in appropriate disciplinary or contractual action, in accordance with Estech's employment policies and applicable law.

 Serious or deliberate violations may be escalated to senior management, legal counsel, law enforcement, or relevant regulatory authorities where appropriate.

 ## 24\. Policy Review

 The Cybersecurity Policy shall be reviewed **at least annually** and whenever there are significant:

 - Changes to Estech's technology environment.
- Changes to applicable laws or regulations.
- Changes to business operations.
- Cybersecurity incidents.
- Changes in the threat environment.

 Updates shall be approved by the appropriate management authority.

 ## 25\. Employee Acknowledgement

 All employees and relevant contractors are required to acknowledge that they have:

 - Read and understood this policy.
- Agreed to comply with its requirements.
- Understood their responsibility to protect Estech's information and technology resources.
- Agreed to report suspected cybersecurity incidents promptly.
